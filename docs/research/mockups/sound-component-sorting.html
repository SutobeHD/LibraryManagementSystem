Sort by sound components — wireframe

Planned UI for Option A of docs/research/implement/review_sound-component-sorting.md: bass, hats and song-structure values plus "sort by similarity", all without a new dependency. Track names are placeholders. Numbers in cells are illustrative; the ranges and costs in the notes are measured on the real library.
1

Collection with component columns switched on

New columns live in the existing column picker and are hidden by default. They load only when one of them is visible. Cells show nicht analysiert before a scan; columns that passed only the "coarse" stability bar carry a marker. Sorting puts empty cells last.

2

Right-click → "Nach Ähnlichkeit sortieren"

One entry with four kinds. Bass compares the bass sound on a pitch-relative scale (a transposed copy still matches) plus the bass line; "mit Kick" includes the kick. Hats compare the high-end timbre and decay. Aufbau compares the phrase build-up within the same Rekordbox mood.

4

Settings → Analysis: two scans

Nothing runs on its own. The structure scan reads Rekordbox's phrase data only (no audio decode); the audio scan analyses the middle two minutes of every track in the background. Both can be cancelled and resume where they stopped; values appear while they run.

5

Structure tooltip + family filter

Hovering the structure bar shows the phrase sequence in bars. Families are simple rules (number of breaks × when the main part starts), not a learned clustering — the measurement found no stable natural clusters.
